# K230 serial notes

K230 protocol varies by firmware. Typical settings used in many projects:
- Baud: 115200 (or 9600 depending on your device)
- 8N1

Example simple command frame (ASCII) used in other projects: \"GOTO 45.0\n\"

If you need binary protocol to talk to a K230 module, provide specific frame format and I'll add a parser.
